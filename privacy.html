Privacy policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Solo Leveling is a personal assistant bot used only by its owner. It has no other users.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, it reads the owner's Google Calendar events and adds events the owner has approved.

How data is used

Calendar data is used only to remind the owner about their own schedule. It is not sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Storage and removal

Access can be removed at any time from the Google Account's third-party access settings.
